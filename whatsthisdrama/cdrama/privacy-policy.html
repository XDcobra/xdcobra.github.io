2. Overview

What's This Cdrama (package / bundle ID: com.whatsthisdrama.cdrama) is a mobile app that helps you find titles by describing a plot, mood, or scene. Search runs against our title catalog and backend services. You can browse and search with an anonymous installation; signing in is optional and unlocks a higher monthly search allowance and cloud-synced favorites.

This privacy policy explains which data is processed when you use the app and for which purposes.

3. Data collection and processing

3.1 Installation identifier and local preferences

When you first use the app, we register an installation with our backend and issue an opaque access token. The token is stored securely on your device (iOS Keychain / Android equivalent). It is not your name or email. It identifies this install for authentication, rate limiting, and monthly search quotas.

Locally, the app may also store preferences (for example appearance and language settings) and cached data needed for offline-friendly UI. These preferences stay on your device unless a feature explicitly syncs them.

3.2 Search queries and usage quota

When you search, the app sends your search text and optional filter parameters to our backend over HTTPS. We process these requests to return ranked title results (titles, metadata, short plot/description text we provide, artwork URLs where available) and to count searches against your monthly quota.

We do not sell your search queries. We may retain technical logs (timestamps, install/account identifiers, success/error status) for security, abuse prevention, debugging, and operating quotas.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, where applicable, our legitimate interest in fair use limits and abuse prevention (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.3 Optional account sign-in (Logto / Google)

You may optionally link an account via our identity provider (Logto) using Google sign-in. If you do, we receive identity claims needed to link the account to your installation (for example a stable subject identifier and, if provided by the identity provider, a display name).

Signed-in users receive an unlimited (or otherwise elevated) monthly search allowance as configured in the product, and may sync favorites to our backend associated with that account.

Legal basis: your consent / contract performance when you choose to sign in (Art. 6(1)(a) and/or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Logto / Google process authentication data under their own policies. Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.4 Favorites

If you save favorites, the app stores title identifiers (and related cache needed to show posters/titles) on your device and, when you are signed in, may sync them to our backend so you can restore them across devices.

3.5 Artwork and third-party content hosts

Title artwork and similar media may be loaded from third-party CDN or image hosts. Your device connects directly to those hosts; they may process IP address and standard request metadata according to their own policies.

3.6 Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process the content of your message and the contact details you provide in order to respond.

4. Permissions

The app may use:

  • Internet access — to call our backend, load artwork, and complete sign-in.
  • Network status — to detect connectivity where helpful for UX.

Permissions can be reviewed in your device settings.

5. Disclosure of data to third parties

We disclose personal data only as needed to operate the app:

  • Our hosting / backend infrastructure — to run search, auth, quota, and favorites APIs.
  • Logto / Google — if you choose to sign in (section 3.3).
  • Image / CDN hosts — when the app loads artwork (section 3.5).
  • Legal obligations — where we are required to disclose data to authorities.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Storage periods

  • Installation tokens and quota records — retained while the installation is active and for a reasonable period afterward for security and abuse prevention; you may request deletion (section 8).
  • Account-linked data — retained while your account is linked; deleted or anonymized when you delete your account in-app (where available) or upon a valid erasure request.
  • Local device data — until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Email correspondence — as long as needed to handle your request and meet statutory retention duties.

8. Account and installation deletion

Sign out disconnects this device from your account without deleting it (Account → Sign out). Synced favorites remain on the account.

Delete account permanently removes your login and account-linked data we control (favorites, account quota records). In the app: Account → Legal → Delete account (confirm). Profile and two-factor authentication are managed via Manage account (identity provider); deletion is not offered there. You may also email us to request erasure of installation- or account-linked data. Some technical logs may be retained for a limited time where required for security or legal compliance.
